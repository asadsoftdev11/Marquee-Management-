The Marquee Management System is designed to simplify and digitalize the complete workflow of event venue operations. It provides a centralized platform where administrators can manage marquee details, customer records, and bookings with ease.

The system ensures efficient scheduling of events and smooth coordination between different operational modules such as Marquees, Bookings, Menu Categories, and Menu Items.
